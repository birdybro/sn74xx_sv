# ttl_74x1074 — Dual D negative-edge-triggered flip-flop with /PRE and /CLR

Negative-edge-triggered relative of the standard
[ttl_74x74](../ttl_74x74/README.md): each of the two flops captures D
on the *falling* edge of its own clock. Async `/PRE` and `/CLR` follow
the standard convention (clear priority over preset). Both Q and
Q-complement outputs are provided per unit.
